Privacy policy

This policy covers the Tacos and Beer website and our three restaurants in New Orleans, Slidell and Hammond, Louisiana.

What we collect

  • When you book a table or ask about a group: your name, phone number, the date, time and size of your party, and your email and any notes if you give them.
  • If you tick the offers box: that you said yes, the words you agreed to, and when.

We do not ask for payment details on this site.

How we use it

  • To hold your table and reach you if something changes.
  • To send Taco Tuesday news, events and offers by email, only if you ticked the box.

Who sees it

Your booking goes to our staff through the group chat and calendar they use to run the restaurant (GroupMe), and it is stored by the services that run this site and send our email (Cloudflare and Resend). They handle it for us and for nothing else. We do not sell or rent your information. We share it with others only if the law requires it.

Offers and how to stop

The offers box starts empty, and leaving it empty does not affect your booking. Every email we send has an unsubscribe link, and you can also call us and ask to be taken off.

Your choices

Call us to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, and we will tell you if it is ever exposed, as Louisiana law requires.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Bookings depend on availability. To change or cancel, call the location you booked. We may release a table if your party is late. Group requests are confirmed only once we call you back.
